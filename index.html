Comprehensive Facilities

Designed for Complete Peace of Mind

Every amenity has been curated to ensure high-class comfort for the hosts and a memorable experience for every guest.

High-Power Central AC

State-of-the-art chiller systems maintaining a crisp, refreshing temperature throughout the main hall, dining area, and lobbies.

Luxury Stay Suites

Stay right upstairs! Luxurious air-conditioned suites with plush bedding, attached modern bathrooms, and full room service.

100% DG Power Backup

Dual heavy-duty silent generators ensuring uninterrupted power for air-conditioning, stage lighting, and sound systems.

Spacious Parking & Valet

Ample covered and open parking capacity for 150+ cars and 300+ two-wheelers, supported by courteous valet drivers.

Dedicated Bridal Suites

Well-lit bridal and groom green rooms equipped with vanity mirrors, garment steaming space, and private restrooms.

Modern Kitchen Facilities

Huge commercial-grade preparation kitchen with RO water purification, separate veg and non-veg washing, and cooking bays.

24/7 CCTV & Security

Full premises monitored with high-resolution CCTV security cameras and on-site trained security personnel at all gates.
